he yangs (阳) of the sola district is one of the most prestigious clans of the entirety of luminae. one of adrian's ancestors had been one of the seven founding elders of luminae, who had worked directly under rosalind luminaria. They live in the penthouse in fleur de sol street among other families of the seven founding elders of luminae.

at luminaria academy, the yang siblings continue to keep up their appearances. but it begins to prove difficult when the security tightens around the sola district after the lalunians began to fight back against the very-open discrimination against them.

however, when the current head of the yangs went to the great woe and never came back, the matriach hung herself in the same penthouse condo that adrian and his siblings still resides in, the yangs seemed to have lost their light. 
about adrian
unlike his elder brother, adrian does not have to balance between being the new head of the yangs and being a young man. in his brother's shadow, he was able to fool around while taking care of only the loose matters that his elder brother accidentally let slip. he's normally a bit of a goofball, but can have an unmovingly uptight side to him.


a/n on geeking out about his chinese name

his chinese name is yang shao qiong 阳绍穹 came to me really quickly. the standalone characters can be roughly translated to "sun" "connect/ introduce" "sky", which connects with the idea that the solansk believe that only with light will there be sky. The idea is that his parents would have enjoyed the idea of naming their second child after their belief than name him something nice like the names i thought of for his siblings, both of which were a reflection of their excellence (劭悌： “strength", "respect for one's brother/ gentle/ intimate"; and 昭娟: "bright", "graceful/ pretty")

additionally and interestingly, while using a hanja dictionary, it seems 绍 also means "loose/ lax", which seems to be the perfect juxtaposition between the image his parents wanted for him, and the image he seems to have made. the word can also be used in the chinese context of "continuing/ inheriting," which is interesting to me personally since i had intended for adrian to be a middle child, whose entire personality is established on attempting to be neutral—the middle ground

his timeline
 
31 august 2001
adrian was born to welt and agatha yang (née kaslana).
 
2006–2012
adrian attended classes at the sola elite academy of the young (seaty).

in seaty, he was able to impress with his seemingly innate ability to harness and manipulate light.
 
2013–2020
adrian attended the sola elite academy (sea). He performed well-above average, but was not quite as excellent as his brilliant and bright brothers.
 
2020
in early 2020, when the great woe occured, adrian lost his father in the war.

following his father's death, in late 2020, his mother hung herself in their penthouse condo due to depression. 

adrian realised that there was wiggle room for him to fool around while his elder brother held the fort. he was only as useful as his brother is dead.
 
2021–present
adrian was accepted into the sola academy of higher education (sahe) and is currently studying there as a fifth year student.
